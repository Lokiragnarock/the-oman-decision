## CLOs
- CLO1: international financial markets and monetary systems (L4).
- CLO2: interpret the BoP and its impact (L5).
- CLO3: apply exchange rate theories.
- CLO4: evaluate FX exposure and recommend hedges.
- CLO5: international capital budgeting.

## Assessment
CIA 1 20 marks (counts 20), CIA 2 mid-sem 50 marks (counts 25), CIA 3 20 marks (counts 20), ESE 50 marks (counts 30), attendance 5.

### CIA 1: Global Finance Crisis Room (due 30 Nov 2026)
- Teams of 3-4. Report on Google Classroom (rubric out of 20, converted to 15) plus a class presentation (5).
- Scenario: an Indian MNC plans to invest abroad amid FX uncertainty and a host-country BoP imbalance.
- Tasks: (1) why invest or borrow in that market; (2) fixed vs flexible regimes and how the monetary system evolved; (3) host country BoP, drivers of the current account and the capital/financial account; (4) proceed, postpone or modify, with reasons.
- Deliverables: a report of about 1,000-1,200 words, one visual (BoP dashboard, monetary-system timeline or country chart), and a presentation or viva.
- Data: India plus one other economy, or a faculty-given case. Sources: RBI, IMF, World Bank.
- Rubric (4 criteria, 1-5 each): markets and monetary systems (CLO1); BoP interpretation (CLO2); economic conditions and business implications (CLO2); evidence-based recommendation.

### CIA 2: mid-sem
Individual, handwritten case study, Units 1-3, per the central timetable.

### CIA 3: Global Treasury War Room (due 18 Feb 2027)
- Teams of 3-4, report (15) plus presentation (5).
- Scenario: an Indian MNC builds a UK plant, buys machinery in USD, borrows in a foreign currency and earns GBP.
- Task A: cross rates, bid-ask, arbitrage, PPP, IRP, Fisher, spot vs forward.
- Task B: exposures; forwards, futures, options, swaps, balance sheet hedge, invoicing, leading and lagging; recommend a hedge.
- Task C: project cash flows, NPV by currency approach, APV if the data allows, political risk; invest, postpone or reject.
- Deliverables: a report of 1,500-2,000 words, an Excel workbook and a one-page dashboard.
- Rubric: FX calculations and theory; exposure and hedging; project appraisal; integrated recommendation and political risk.

### ESE (50, 2 hours)
Section A 3 of 5 x 5 (either/or); Section B 2 of 3 x 10 (either/or); Section C compulsory case 15.
