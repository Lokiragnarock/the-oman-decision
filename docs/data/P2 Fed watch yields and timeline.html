Tangent T6 · Packet P2 · Feeds C1, C3

Oman copied every Fed move since July 2022, so the rial's rate is the Fed's rate plus 50bp. A Fed hike on 9 Dec is priced at about 95%.

CBO repo is 4.50% (Fed range 3.75-4.00%, upper bound 4.00% + 0.50). The 1y USD/OMR forward is quoted within about 0.1% of spot, so the rate gap is not what a rial hedge costs today. The rupee forward premium is 3.4 to 3.5% a year (May 2026), well above the 1.5 point India-US bill gap.

Case: Indian company deciding whether to proceed, postpone or modify an Oman investment (ACME Duqm phases 2 and 3, or OMIFCO). Compiled 24 Nov 2026, online sources only, no figure from memory. Reuses the CBO 4.50%, Fed 3.75-4.00%, RBI 5.50%, USD/OMR 0.3850 and USD/INR 92.84 already pulled in CIA Crisis Room Deck.md and the class notes Exchange Rate System, Bimetallism, IMF. Five findings

  1. Oman has matched all 15 documented Fed moves since July 2022, the same size, 12 of them on the same day. The other three (Jul, Sep, Nov 2022) came 2 to 3 days later. CBO's own rule is repo = upper bound of the Fed range + 50bp: 4.00 + 0.50 = 4.50. S4 Table A
  2. Before 2022 the link was looser, and the record is thin. Of 17 Fed moves from Dec 2015 to Mar 2022, I found one dated CBO match (Mar 2020, a 75bp cut to 0.50%), one explicit refusal (Dec 2015: "no intention to change the official interest rate"), and 15 with no dated CBO decision found. The 50bp spread rule is documented only from 2022. S9 S11
  3. Oman's premium over the US is small and shrinking. The Nov 2025 USD sukuk priced at +60bp over Treasuries (7.5y, 4.525%). IMF staff put average sovereign spreads at 129bp in Jan to Oct 2025, down from 310bp in 2022. 5y CDS was about 76bp in Aug 2026. A current secondary-market USD bond yield was not found, so the sovereign premium is shown at issue, not live. Table B
  4. The rial's hedge cost is not the rate gap. The indicative 1y USD/OMR forward is -10 / +25 points (about -0.03% / +0.07% of spot). A pure rate-gap calculation (Oman 364d bill 3.80% vs UST 1y 4.47%) would imply about -0.64%, roughly -250 points. The market prices peg credibility, not the gap. In Feb 2016 the same forward hit 1,500 points (about 4%). The rupee is the opposite: premium 3.37 to 3.54% a year, versus a 1.49 point gap. Table C
  5. Another US hike is next, and both central banks follow. FOMC decides 9 Dec 2026 (next 27 Jan 2027). Futures price a 25bp hike at about 88.6 to 96% (sources differ). On CBO's own rule that takes repo to 4.75%. RBI meets 2 to 4 Dec (decision 4 Dec; Trading Economics consensus 5.75%). Oman's regime is a conventional peg (rial at 2.6008 USD since 1986); India is floating de jure and crawl-like de facto since 28 Nov 2025. Table A Table D
