Ammonia and urea prices: the scenario simulator should treat both as gas and war driven, not as a stable base

Global Business Finance CIA, ACME Duqm green ammonia (phase 2) against OMIFCO urea. All sources fetched and read on 24 Nov 2026. Prices in USD per metric tonne unless stated.

Headline. Urea (Middle East, World Bank) averaged USD 423/t in 2025 and USD 531/t over Jan to Oct 2026, peaking at USD 857/t in Apr 2026 before falling back to USD 420/t in Oct. Grey ammonia followed the same shock: Tampa settled at USD 645/t for Nov 2026 after a USD 825/t peak in May, and NW Europe was USD 710 to 730/t on 2 Nov. The India auction price for green ammonia, USD 641/t (Jul 2025), is now within 1% of Tampa grey, so the green premium is close to zero at today's war-elevated grey prices but about 25% against the pre-war grey price of USD 515/t.

Five findings

  1. Urea and ammonia track gas, not oil. Over 2014 to 2025 (12 annual points), the Middle East urea price correlates 0.96 with World Bank European gas, 0.82 with Henry Hub and 0.67 with Brent; US Gulf ammonia shows 0.96, 0.87 and 0.73. This is my own calculation and the 2022 gas spike drives it. Estimated
  2. 2026 is a supply shock year. Urea went from USD 415/t (Jan) to USD 857/t (Apr) and back to USD 390/t (Aug). Reports tie the move to the Strait of Hormuz disruption. The simulator range must be wide. Verified for urea, Reported for the cause
  3. Annual ammonia data is thin in the open. Only the USGS US Gulf Coast average is freely published for 2014 to 2025. Middle East and NW Europe annual averages are behind Argus, Platts and CRU paywalls: "not found". Gap
  4. Green ammonia sells near USD 590 to 640/t in India, with a wide spread. SECI auctions cleared at USD 591 and USD 641/t in mid 2025, against a first H2Global tender at USD 1,153/t. The IEA puts electrolytic production cost at about three times conventional (2024 basis). No disclosed price exists for the Yara to ACME Oman offtake. Reported
  5. CBAM covers ammonia and has been definitive since 1 Jan 2026. Ammonia (CN 2814) is in the fertiliser list with a transitional default of 2.82 tCO2e per tonne. This raises the landed cost of grey ammonia into the EU and helps green ammonia from Oman. Verified

CBAM coverage

Yes. The Commission states CBAM covers cement, iron and steel, aluminium, fertilisers, electricity and hydrogen and that "as of 1 January 2026, CBAM is applicable under its definitive regime" (src 23). The Commission's transitional default values table lists ammonia, CN 2814, at 2.68 direct plus 0.14 indirect, 2.82 tCO2e per tonne (src 24). Verified For the definitive period, a Commission default-value package carries a 1% markup on fertiliser defaults and is set in Implementing Regulation 2025/2621, with a correction 2026/1740 published July 2026 (src 25, Reported; I did not read the regulation text). Preliminary default costs run up to USD 196/t for US-origin ammonia against about USD 96/t for Trinidad (src 26, Reported). Oman default value: not found. Platts clarified that CBAM costs are not in its existing CFR NW Europe assessment, and carries a separate CBAM-paid series (src 12, 27).
