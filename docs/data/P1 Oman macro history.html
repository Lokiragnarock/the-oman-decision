Oman ran a budget deficit of 19.6% of GDP in 2016 and 15.7% in 2020, public debt went from 4.0% to 67.9% of GDP, and the rial peg never moved

Oman macro history 2014 to 2026 for the Global Finance Crisis Room case. Sourced online, fetched 24 Nov 2026. Where sources disagree both numbers are shown. "not found" means no source located; nothing is estimated. Tags: Verified primary read directly, Reported credible secondary, Estimated own arithmetic, Gap missing. Source numbers [S1] link to the annexure at the bottom.

Five findings

1. Debt rose from 4.0% to 67.9% of GDP in six years (2014 to 2020), then halved to 34.7% by 2025. General government debt, IMF WEO. The 2014 to 2016 oil collapse drove most of the build-up; the 2022 to 2025 surpluses (10.3%, 6.7%, 3.3%, 0.6% of GDP) paid it down. [S4][S3]
2. In 2016 and 2020 Brent averaged $45 and $43 against an IMF fiscal breakeven of $100 and $86; in 2025 the gap flipped to +$11. Oman's budget needed about double the market price in both crises. The 2025 cushion is thin: breakeven $57 vs Brent $68. The 2026 breakeven was not found. [S6][S7]
3. S&P took Oman from A to B+ (8 notches) between Dec 2014 and Oct 2020; by Dec 2025 all three agencies had it back at investment grade. S&P BBB- Sep 2024, Moody's Baa3 Jul 2025, Fitch BBB- Dec 2025. The sequence shows a market that punished Oman fast on oil and rewarded it slowly on repayment. [S12][S13]
4. Reserves never fell below US$15.0bn (2020 low), but the 2016 peak of US$20.3bn dropped 21% to US$16.1bn within a year. World Bank total reserves incl. gold. Import cover was 3.7 months in 2024 (World Bank) or 4.2 months of next-year imports (IMF), against 4.5 to 7.1 months in 2014 to 2021. Oman borrowed and drew buffers; it did not touch the peg. [S8][S9][S5]
5. Growth is oil-led again: Q2 2026 real GDP +5.1% y/y, but oil +14.7% and non-oil only +0.7%. Contractor payment arrears were documented in 2016 and delays again noted in 2020, but no total was found. GCC money was a 2011 pledge, not a crisis rescue; Qatar's reported US$1bn in Oct 2020 is unconfirmed. [S17b][S20][S21][S26]
