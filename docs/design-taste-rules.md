Built from the user's built Canva slides (pp.1-10, 2026-10-18), saved references (A1-A3, M1-M5, N1-N2) and his
corrections. **The built slides are the artifact; where they disagree with an older spec, the built slides win.**

## Corrections (his words, strongest signal)
- R1 Drafts are drawing material: no status tags, OPEN boxes, appendix pointers, role bands or question boxes.
  "Don't make me filter through noise." (2026-10-19)
- R2 Draw the layout he describes, literally. R3 v1 swapped his three-results layout for a scatter he found
  meaningless. (2026-10-19)
- R3 Build only the named element; no unasked re-cuts. (memory: build-only-what-asked)

- R22 **Draw in his Canva visual language, not the draft style.** (2026-10-19: "your shit is ass. where's all the
  taste skill".) Use `deck/canva.css`: serif page number and deck line, yellow vertical bar and top rule, EY mark,
  Open Sans 800 caps title, Poppins section heads with a 5 px rule (grey, dark or yellow), yellow pills, dark rounded
  cards with a yellow dot, golden harvey balls, dashed row separators, slate month chips. `draft.css` is retired.
- R23 **"Make it better" means improve his slide in his style, never replace it with a new layout.** Unbuilt slides
  come before any re-imagining of built ones (2026-10-19).

- R24 **Risk mitigation = Gantt on the rollout grid** (user, 2026-10-19). Rows = risks ordered by start, columns =
  months under wave chips; grey band = risk live, yellow pill = action that closes it, dashed pill = fallback watched,
  dark diamond = gate or go/no-go, dark KPI pill at row end. Pattern from practitioner roadmap guides (Deckary,
  Slideworks): bars by start date, milestones distinct from bars, gates as labelled decision points.

- R25 **Clean variant, kept beside R22** (user, 2026-10-20: "not sharp, too many coloured boxes, not clean as I would
  like it to be"; keep both versions). `deck/clean.css`: same chrome; no filled pills, chevrons or dark cards; labels
  are small-caps text; columns split by hairlines; harvey balls in ink; yellow marks the answer only. Every redesign is
  drafted in both (A = R22, B = R25) until he picks one per slide. `FRAME` until his Canva redraw settles it.

## Read from the assets (what recurs)
- R4 **Values sit on the marks.** Numbers inside bars or beside dots, no axis needed to read them (M1, M2, M5, his
  waterfalls on Benefits and Pods).
- R5 **One highlight marks the answer.** Everything else grey or dark; the proposed option gets the colour (his NL
  column in yellow, M5's single light column, M1's "Yes" bar).
- R6 **Group by space, not by boxes in boxes.** Columns under a coloured tier bar (M3), rows split by dashed rules
  (M2). Thin rules and white space do the grouping.
- R7 **Short text beside each visual row, not paragraphs under it** (M2 "Key drivers", N1 timeline notes, his
  Problem Statement driver column).
- R8 **Chart subtitle names the measure and unit** in one line: "Global market size, $ billion" (M1, M2, M5).
- R9 **Big number, small caption** for KPIs (A1 "35-40%", his Benefits pills, his 75+ / 25+ callouts).
- R10 **Option comparison = columns with a highlighted column and harvey balls** (his Hub in the Netherlands slide,
  his bank grid on Industry context).
- R11 **Sequence = timeline or chevrons with month or step stamps** (his TMS Structure grid, Next acquisition Gantt,
  N1 exit timeline, his chevrons on Industry context).
- R12 **Tiers by tint of one colour** (A2 nested yellows); **flows by filled blocks with white text** (A3).
- R13 **One dark banner line states the "so what"** under a panel (his Benefits and NL hub slides, N1's boxed
  probability-weighted paragraph).
- R14 **Constant chrome:** page number + deck name + yellow rule top, EY logo top right, yellow rule above the
  sources line, section strip bottom.

- R17 **Panel = question, big answer, one line, then evidence** (N3). Each column opens with its question as a grey
  subheading, the answer in large colour ("Late Q2"), one line of reasoning, then small charts. The question is
  content inside the panel; a separate meta box ("the question this slide must answer") is noise (R1).
- R18 **Before vs after as paired marks per row** (N3): this period above, comparison below, values on the bars.
- R19 **Ratio between two marks, written on the connector** (N3: two circles sized by value, "~1x" / ">4x" between).
- R20 **Ranges shown as ranges** (N4): box = middle range, whisker = wide range, tick = median, one colour, sorted.
  Matches the user's rule that forecasts are ranges, not points (2026-10-10).
- R21 **Columns split by thin vertical rules, no panel fills** (N3). His Canva uses fills; both appear, no rule yet.
